# ADR-000: Template

**Status:** template
**Date:** —
**Supersedes:** —
**Superseded by:** —

## Context
What forced a decision. State the constraint, not the preference.

## Options
Every option that was seriously considered, with its cost.

## Decision
One sentence. What was chosen.

## Consequences
What becomes easy. What becomes hard. What is now impossible without revisiting
this record.

## Validation
How we will know the decision was right or wrong. A decision with no way to be
proven wrong is a preference, not a decision.
