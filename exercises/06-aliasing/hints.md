Aliasing is not "holds an equal value". It is "names the same object".

In the OBJECTS panel, aliasing looks like two slots in FRAMES carrying the SAME
number: `a -> [2]` and `b -> [2]`. Two objects would be `[2]` and `[3]`, however
equal their contents.

This exercise has a wrong solution that prints the right answer. That is the
whole reason the picture exists.
